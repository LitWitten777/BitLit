I audited every Lean theorem under `formal/` and produced a report identifying the pseudo-theorems, plus machine-checked evidence that they are empty. Of the 2 344 theorems across the 145 modules, 727 (31%) are closed arithmetic identities about hard-coded constants — statements whose only content is something like `8 + 3 + 3 = 14`, with the physics or number theory present only in identifier names and docstrings; a further 432 (18%) are closed finite evaluations of the project's own definitions. Thirteen modules consist of nothing but numeral identities (`HigherTower`, `TrigintaduonionLDPC`, `TrigintaduonionCSS`, `SideChannelResistance`, `ChingonSpectrum`, `QuantumThresholdObstruction`, `BoundaryCoupling`, `LiePoissonIntegrator`, `CoupledSingularity`, `ABCDensityObstruction`, `HigherDimensionProverCampaign`, `UnifiedOrganismPipeline`, `TowerOrganismSystem`), and 41 are at least half such. Four recurring failure modes are documented: the claim living in the `def` rather than the theorem (empirical counts and "exact" invariants asserted by fiat, then re-proved by `rfl`); ground arithmetic with a grand name; outright tautologies (`x = x ∧ ¬(x ≠ x)`, `¬∃P, P ∧ ¬P`, trichotomy, and conjuncts `already_proved_theorem = rfl`, true by proof irrelevance); and generic analysis facts carrying a famous name. The modules that do contain real mathematics are listed too, so the report is balanced. Deliverables: `PSEUDO_THEOREM_AUDIT.md` (taxonomy with worked examples, a verdict for each of the ~65 theorems named `…settled`/`…falsified`/`…certified`/`…master`, a per-module table, methodology, limits and remediation advice); `audit/pseudo_theorem_inventory.csv` classifying all 2 344 theorems, regenerable with `audit/classify_theorems.py` together with `audit/module_table.md`; and `formal/PseudoTheoremAudit.lean`, which makes the audit verifiable by restating the ground proposition each headline claim really is and closing it with `exact <the original theorem>` — an elaboration that succeeds only when the two statements are literally the same proposition. It certifies, among others, that the Yang–Mills "mass gap settled" theorem is `8+3+3=14 ∧ 14−3=11 ∧ 1+10=11 ∧ 2·5=10` plus positivity of `√(11/8)`; that the Navier–Stokes "global regularity master theorem" is `3 = 3 ∧ 598 > 100 ∧ 598−100 = 498`; that the Riemann "master certification" is `1/2 = 1/2` plus a positivity fact, the zero being constructed with real part 1/2; that the CSS "falsification refuted" theorem is the law of non-contradiction and the gauge-unification one is `0 = 0 ∧ ¬(0 ≠ 0)`; and that the Yang–Mills positivity half holds with the curvature constant replaced by an arbitrary positive real. The new module builds with no `sorry` and only standard kernel axioms, and the whole project still builds; no existing file was modified apart from adding the module to the build list, and nothing was deleted. The audit found no false theorem: everything that compiles is true — "pseudo" here means vacuous relative to its name.
