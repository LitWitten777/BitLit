### Workstream W22: Spectral Number Field Stabilization Tower $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{57})$
* **Goal**: Establish the algebraic number field generated by regular sector quotient spectra across the doubling tower.
* **Key Invariants**:
  - For $D \le 32$: All equitable quotient eigenvalues are rational integers $\mathbb{Z}$, splitting completely over $\mathbb{Q}$.
  - In $D=64$: The characteristic polynomial of regular sectors contains an irreducible quadratic factor $P_{64}(x) = x^2 + 4x - 224$ over $\mathbb{Q}$.
    $$\Delta(64) = 4^2 - 4(1)(-224) = 16 + 896 = 912 = 16 \times 57$$
    Roots: $x = -2 \pm 4\sqrt{57} \implies$ splitting field is $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{57})$.
  - In $D=128$: The characteristic polynomial contains an irreducible factor $P_{128}(x) = x^2 - 912$.
    $$\Delta(128) = 0^2 - 4(1)(-912) = 3648 = 64 \times 57 \implies \text{Roots } \pm 4\sqrt{57} \implies \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{57})$$
