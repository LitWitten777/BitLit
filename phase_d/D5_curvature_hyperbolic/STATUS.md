## Deliverables

1. **Hyperbolic factor** `d5_curvature/hyperbolic.py`: `TreeBall(d, R)` (exact
   words, closed-form distance) and `TilingBall(p, q, R)` (hyperboloid frames,
   edge length `2·arccosh(cos(π/p)/sin(π/q))`, faces traced from the rotation
   system). Exact distance and routing in the finite ball (`distance`,
   `greedy_next`, `shortest_path`), sphere/ball sizes. Checks: `q`-regularity,
   every interior vertex in `q` faces, every edge in ≤ 2 faces, hyperbolic
   edge lengths, `{3,7}` sphere sizes `1, 7, 21, 56, 147`, exponential growth.
2. **Product** `d5_curvature/product.py`: `ProductSpace(n, moduli, balls)` with
   `size`, `vertices`, `neighbors`, `step`, `distance`, routing; local
   `HAddress`/`HEdgeLabel` and adapter; agrees with
   `ws1_geometry.MixedRadixSpace` when there is no hyperbolic factor.
3. **Curvature** `d5_curvature/curvature.py` (+ `cell_complex.py`): exact
   `W₁` by integer min-cost flow; `ollivier` (`κ_α`), `lly` (`2·κ_{1/2}`,
   checked against `κ_α/(1−α)` at four values of `α`), `forman_graph`,
   `forman` (2-complex).
   * `Q_n`: `κ_LLY = 2/n`, Forman `2`.
   * `T^k_m`: `κ_LLY = 0` for `m ≥ 6`; **exceptions** `m = 3, 4, 5` give
     `3/(2k), 1/k, 1/(2k) > 0` (so the flat range is `m ≥ 6`, not `m ≥ 5`);
     Forman `0` for all `m ≥ 3`.
   * `{p,q}` interior: `κ_LLY < 0` (observed closed form `c_p/q − 2`,
     `c_3, c_4, c_5, c_{≥6} = 12, 8, 6, 4`; not proved), Forman
     `16 − 2p − 2q < 0`.
   * Plain graph Forman `4 − d_u − d_v` and non-lazy `κ_0` do **not** separate
     the three families (documented).
4. **Capacity table** `docs/CAPACITY.md`, generated by `d5_curvature/capacity.py`.
5. **Tests** `tests/test_d5_hyperbolic.py`, `test_d5_curvature.py`,
   `test_d5_product.py`, with hypothesis properties mirroring P1.1–P1.5 for the
   balls and P1.1–P1.4 for the product.

## Limits / open

* **Stretch (Ricci flow on edge weights): not done.**
* Ball metric = induced-subgraph metric; distances between two boundary
  vertices can exceed their distance in the infinite tiling (documented in
  `hyperbolic.py`). Curvature is only reported on interior edges, where this
  cannot matter.
* Floating point is used only to identify tiling vertices; radii used here are
  small (coordinates `< 10⁴`). Very large radii would need exact arithmetic.
* The LLY closed form for `{p,q}` and the product scaling are observed on the
  tested instances only.
* Contract: unchanged; FA-020 is a draft awaiting the owner.
